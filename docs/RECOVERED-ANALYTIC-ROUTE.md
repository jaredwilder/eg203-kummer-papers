`jaredwilder/unpublished-math-papers/eg203-analytic-route/`

## Why they belong here

These files are part of the same Kummer/conductor/distribution program as the paper series in this repository. Their status varies, so they should not be silently inserted into the principal-paper list as though all five were finished manuscripts.

The correct organization is:

- finished paper series: `papers/` in this repository;
- recovered analytic subseries: the five sources above, linked here with their historical state intact;
- finite prime-fibre obstruction route: `jaredwilder/erdos203`;
- smaller obstruction-calculus/formal support collection: `jaredwilder/erdos203-obstruction-calculus`.

## Editorial corrections made at first public extraction

The recovered source copies had two non-mathematical cleanup classes recorded before publication:

1. language-model names appearing in author lines, section headings, lemma titles and labels were removed as tooling/session artifacts rather than authorship;
2. references to a draft, unfiled patent-family methodology were removed because they described process rather than mathematics.

The mathematical content was not intentionally altered by those editorial removals.

## Research significance

The most substantial recovered file is `R682-GAMMA-FIBER-LOCAL-DENSITY.tex`, which develops exact Gamma-fiber local density and a Kummer-character remainder. `R705` and `R712` document attempts to cross specific analytic gaps rather than generic brainstorming. Even where unfinished, these files are part of the mathematical dependency history and should be discoverable from the main paper series rather than hidden in a general-purpose archive.
